CURRICULUM DEVELOPED AND TAUGHT

Natural language processing, end to end.

A complete learning progression from statistical language foundations to modern pretrained models, with implementation, evaluation, interpretability, and responsible use treated as one connected discipline.

14-week curriculum and 10-module online sequence
  1. 01

    Language foundations

    NLU and NLG, lexical processing, regular expressions, normalization, corpora, N-grams, probabilistic language models, text classification, and information retrieval.

  2. 02

    Structure and meaning

    Sequence tagging, HMMs, MEMMs, Viterbi decoding, POS tagging, NER, chunking, semantic roles, word sense disambiguation, vector semantics, embeddings, and speech systems.

  3. 03

    Neural sequence models

    Feedforward networks, PyTorch, backpropagation, RNNs, bidirectionality, LSTMs, sentiment analysis, tagging, and text generation.

  4. 04

    Attention and transformers

    Encoder-decoder models, attention, self-attention, multi-head attention, positional encoding, transformer implementation, and interpretability.

  5. 05

    Pretrained models and responsible use

    BERT, GPT, BART, transfer learning, fine-tuning, classification, QA, NER, summarization, discourse, coreference, probing, ethics, and trade-off evaluation.
