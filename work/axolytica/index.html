The context

Most enterprise AI work starts with a customer and a mandate. Axolytica started with an independent product thesis: use AI to turn personal inputs into a more useful, personalized experience. Its live entry point is wellness assessments, reports, and coaching; a broader AI-native social experience remains the longer-term vision. Built since 2025 and still actively operated, the venture connects product direction, full-stack delivery, and the practical work of keeping an application live.

The approach

  • Own the product from thesis to release: define the experience, build the interface and API, and separate the live wellness journey from the longer-term social roadmap.
  • Move the live application to Google Cloud in October 2026: Firebase Hosting and Authentication, a containerized FastAPI service, persistent per-user records, and Cloud Storage for media.
  • Use the right tool for each job: deterministic calculations and a bundled scikit-learn classifier for structured inputs, with Gemini through Vertex AI for structured reports and coaching.
  • Build for continuity beyond the first demo: persist reports and generated outputs, verify identity on protected routes, and reuse saved AI responses rather than regenerate them unnecessarily.
  • Operate and improve the product with regression tests, live functional QA, and responsive browser checks. Keep roadmap features distinct from shipped capabilities and functional checks distinct from clinical or scale validation.

System architecture & flow

  1. 01

    Experience & identity

    A multi-page application on Firebase Hosting, with Firebase Authentication and verified identity on protected FastAPI routes.

  2. 02

    Assessment & generation

    Cloud Run serves deterministic calculations and a bundled scikit-learn classifier; Gemini through Vertex AI produces structured reports and coaching.

  3. 03

    Persistence & continuity

    Firestore stores per-user assessments, reports, and cached AI outputs. Cloud Storage holds avatars and media; existing API media URLs are public-read, not confidential storage.

  4. 04

    Release & operation

    Regression tests, live functional checks, and responsive browser QA support releases. Scale-to-zero hosting controls idle compute, with cold-start and capacity trade-offs.

The founder's perspective

Founder-level ownership across product, engineering, and operations: turning an idea into a live experience, making architecture and cost trade-offs, and continuing to improve it after launch. Alongside enterprise architecture work, Axolytica demonstrates the ability to connect strategy with hands-on execution. It is evidence of building and operating a product, not a claim of product-market fit.
